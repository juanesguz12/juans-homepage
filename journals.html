Journals

Laurel Schwulst - My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be?

My favorite part of this essay were the metaphors she used to describe what a website can be like. My favorite metaphor she uses is how a website is like a room, where you can design it yourself and that it should feel personal to you.

Ursula K. Le Guin - A Rant About "Technology"

One thing that intrigued me was how the foundation of this essay was about science fiction. Most of our popular science fiction is set in the future where technology has expanded so much, so her talking about these simple things that are technology subverts our expectations.

J.R. Carpenter - A Handmade Web

This essay and the first one are very similar in that they talk about how websites shouldn't be polished corporate products, but personalized and real. One thing I like about this website is the amount of dead links shown throughout. I think searching through the links to see which ones are dead and which ones work adds a level of interactivity.
